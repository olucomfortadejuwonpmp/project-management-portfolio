# Construmax Construction Project Experience

**Organization:** Construmax Construction Ltd  
**Period:** June 2020 – June 2021  
**Role:** Construction Officer  
**Industry:** Construction & Building Projects  

## Project Overview

During my time with Construmax Construction Ltd, I was involved in active construction operations, supporting the execution and monitoring of building works from site preparation and foundation activities through structural and blockwork stages.

The experience strengthened my practical understanding of construction execution, site coordination, quality monitoring, construction sequencing, and day-to-day site operations.

## My Role

As a Construction Officer, I worked closely with site teams and construction personnel to support the progress of works and ensure activities were carried out in line with project requirements.

My site responsibilities included:

- Monitoring day-to-day construction activities
- Supporting site coordination and construction planning
- Observing excavation and earthworks
- Monitoring foundation and substructure works
- Checking reinforcement and formwork activities
- Supporting concrete and structural works
- Monitoring blockwork and building construction
- Observing construction quality and workmanship
- Coordinating with site personnel and trades
- Reporting site progress and construction issues
- Supporting site documentation and project records
