# De-Dons Housing Scheme — Project Experience

**Location:** Abuja, Nigeria  
**Period:** June 2021 – April 2022  
**Role:** Technical Consultant  
**Work Arrangement:** Remote  
**Project Type:** Housing Development & Healthcare Facility Planning

---

## Project Overview

I provided remote technical consulting support for housing development activities, including architectural design, technical reviews, development planning, project coordination and documentation.

The work involved supporting management with technical input and coordinating project information remotely.

A major design assignment within the project involved the planning of a proposed healthcare facility.

---

## My Role

My responsibilities included:

- Reviewing project requirements and development activities.
- Providing technical input to management.
- Supporting architectural design and technical decisions.
- Preparing and reviewing architectural layouts.
- Supporting site and development planning.
- Coordinating project-related information remotely.
- Supporting land and development documentation.
- Following up on project activities and requirements.
- Providing technical recommendations to management.

---

## Remote Project Coordination

The assignment was carried out remotely and required clear communication, documentation and regular follow-up.

I supported management by translating project requirements into practical technical and architectural information.

This experience strengthened my ability to coordinate technical work remotely while maintaining communication with project stakeholders.

---

## Architectural & Technical Work

My contribution included:

- Site planning
- Architectural layouts
- Floor plans
- Functional space planning
- Healthcare facility planning
- Residential development planning
- Estate entrance concepts
- Access and circulation considerations
- Technical review
- Development planning
