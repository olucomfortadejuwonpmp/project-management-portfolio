# Tirmana Consult Ltd — Project Experience

**Location:** Abuja, Nigeria  
**Period:** 2014 – Present  
**Role:** Project Manager | Freelance / Project-Based

## Where My Project Management Journey Began

Tirmana Consult Ltd represents an important foundation of my professional career.

This is where I began developing practical experience across architectural work, construction, site activities, technical coordination and project delivery.

Over time, my responsibilities grew beyond technical and site-based work into project planning, coordination, stakeholder communication, problem-solving and project management.

## Working With Different Project Stakeholders

One of the most valuable parts of my experience with Tirmana was learning how to work with different people involved in project delivery.

Depending on the project, I worked with and coordinated:

- Clients
- Architects
- Engineers
- Consultants
- Contractors
- Artisans
- Suppliers and vendors
- Site teams
- Technical personnel
- Other project stakeholders

This experience taught me that successful project delivery is not only about drawings or construction activities. It also depends on communication, coordination, follow-up, problem-solving and making sure different people understand what needs to happen next.

## What I Did

My responsibilities across Tirmana projects included:

- Project planning and coordination
- Construction and site supervision
- Architectural and technical coordination
- Reviewing construction activities against design requirements
- Coordinating contractors, consultants, engineers and artisans
- Monitoring project progress and upcoming activities
- Identifying potential delays and delivery risks
- Following up on corrective actions and outstanding work
- Managing changes to project requirements
- Resolving contractor and artisan performance issues
- Coordinating technical information and design changes
- Supporting regulatory and government requirements
- Communicating project requirements and impacts to stakeholders
- Supporting project documentation and reporting

Tirmana gave me the opportunity to experience project delivery from the practical side.
